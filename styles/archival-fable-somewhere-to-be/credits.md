# Credits: Somewhere to Be

Everything in the film was generated for it or made in code. No stock footage, stock music or recorded sound effects.

## Music

- Three cues generated with ElevenLabs Music v2.5 (`prompts/music.md`). No reference track was used.

## Voice

- ElevenLabs v4 text-to-speech, voice Laura (`prompts/voiceover.md`).

## Sound effects

- Eight effects generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v3 (`prompts/sfx_elevenlabs.md`). No attribution required; no third-party recordings.
