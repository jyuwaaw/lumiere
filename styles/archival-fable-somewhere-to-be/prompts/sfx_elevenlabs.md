# Sound-effect prompts (ElevenLabs Sound Effects v3)

| File | Length | Prompt | Use and result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `projector.mp3` | 30 s, `loop: true` | Vintage 16mm film projector running in a quiet room: soft steady mechanical whir and gentle fast clatter of the film gate, light tape hiss, occasional soft crackle. Even, continuous, no voices. | The bed under the whole film, pulled out entirely for the black. Works |
| `tick.mp3` | 1 s | A single crisp mechanical clock tick, close up, dry, no reverb, one tick only. | The routine clock. Trimmed to the attack, low-passed at 3.5 kHz (it clicked), panned left and right for tick-tock, louder as it speeds up |
| `bolt.mp3` | 2 s | A heavy iron door bolt slammed shut hard, loud metallic clang with a short wooden thud, one hit. | On the bolt shot at 40.6 s and again in the montage. Works |
| `knock.mp3` | 2 s | Two gentle, polite knocks on a solid wooden door, close up, then silence. | The file had three knocks; each one is cut out and its attack put on a measured contact frame of the knocking clip |
| `doors.mp3` | 4 s | Several old wooden doors swinging open one after another down a corridor, soft creaks, airy. | Under the corridor of opening doors. Works |
| `riser.mp3` | 1.2 s | A short cinematic reverse swell riser, building tension over one second, then cut off. No hit at the end. | Ends exactly where the fear cue starts. Works |
| `boom.mp3` | 3 s | A deep cinematic sub boom impact, one hit, with a long low rumble tail. | On the fear cue's first beat. Works |
| `impact1.mp3` | 4 s | A massive cinematic impact hit: deep low boom and orchestral slam, with a huge cathedral reverb tail that rings out and decays to silence over three seconds. One hit only. | The hard cut to black. Its 42 ms pre-roll is trimmed so the attack lands on the cue's last downbeat |

A `roomtone` bed (*Quiet night room tone with faint distant wind, very soft, continuous, no voices, no music.*, 30 s, loop) was made for v1 and is not in the v2 mix.

Lesson: align every effect by its attack, not its peak, and trim its pre-roll first.
